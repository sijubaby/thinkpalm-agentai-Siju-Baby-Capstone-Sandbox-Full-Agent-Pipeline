# Feature: Crew Certification Expiry Monitoring and Alerts

## Domain
Maritime fleet management — crew compliance module

## Actors
- Crew member
- Vessel administrator (DPA)
- Fleet compliance system

## Business rules
1. The system shall send alerts at **90**, **30**, and **7** days before a mandatory certificate expires.
2. When a mandatory certificate is **expired**, the system shall **block embarkation / sign-on** for that crew member.
3. Alerts shall be visible on the crew certification dashboard and sent to the vessel administrator.
4. If multiple mandatory certificates exist, the **earliest expiry** drives alert scheduling.
5. Partial renewal (pending approval) shall show status **PENDING** and shall not unblock embarkation until approved.

## Edge cases
- Certificate crosses expiry at midnight in vessel local timezone
- Crew member holds two mandatory certs with different expiry dates
- Grace period requested but not approved — embarkation remains blocked
- Alert job delayed — system must still block expired embarkation

## Compliance notes
- **COMPLIANCE_EMBARKATION_BLOCK**: Expired mandatory certificate must prevent embarkation (STCW-aligned operational control).
- **COMPLIANCE_ALERT_SCHEDULE**: 90/30/7-day advance alerts are mandatory for audit trail.
- **SAFETY_CRITICAL_SILENT_FAILURE**: System must not allow embarkation without firing expiry alerts when within alert window.
